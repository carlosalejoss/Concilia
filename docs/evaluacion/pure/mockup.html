      Sign-in access

      A group’s View permission determines which main sections appear in the menu. A screen must be available to the role and permitted by its group before it opens. New, Edit, Delete and Print are separate permissions; voucher deletion is never permitted.

      Password strength, failed-login lockout threshold and duration, verified reset procedure and idle-session timeout are to be defined. This prototype does not enforce those undecided protections.

      Operational safeguards and delivery requirements are shown alongside access management so pending decisions are not mistaken for working controls.

      Deletion boundary

      Records, not vouchers

      Voucher records cannot be deleted, even if a group has Delete permission. Try the rejection below; the voucher stock will not change.

      Non-voucher records

      For this demonstration, an administrator with Delete permission can remove an account other than their own, or an unused group, from the lists. These are non-voucher records. Other record types, group-specific deletion rights and deletion after finalization are to be defined; this demonstration is not an approved production deletion policy.
