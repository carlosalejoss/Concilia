1. Introduction
The purpose of LibraryLoan is to reduce the workload of the library staff.
2. Functional requirements
2.1 A member can reserve a book that is on loan.
2.2 When a member returns a book, the system will update the loan record.
2.3 If the barcode of a book cannot be read, the system shall display an error message to the librarian.
2.4 The librarian shall be able to:
- register new members,
- deactivate members.
2.5 The system may send reminder emails before the due date.
2.6 Members can subscribe to the Flexible Loan plan.
3. Non-functional requirements
3.1 The response time of the search function shall be less than 2 seconds.
3.2 In a future release, the system will integrate with the city card.
